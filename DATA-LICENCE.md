# Data provenance and licence

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers **code and the specification only**. The gloss text
has other owners. This page records what is actually known about it, including where an
earlier version of this file was wrong.

**Status: unresolved.** Do not redistribute the gloss text, or audio derived from it,
without checking the terms yourself.

## English word-by-word text

Source: **QUL resource 564**, *Colored English wbw translation* — not resource 92, as
this file previously stated. Resource 92 is a different text (88,244 records, sourced
from the Quran Corpus project); ours has 77,429, matching 564 exactly once the three
`بَعْدَ مَا` splits described below are accounted for.

QUL's record for 564 carries this metadata:

```json
{ "archive-embed-url": "https://archive.org/embed/quran-word-by-word-vol-1" }
```

That item is *"[PDF] Quran word by word English translation (color) Vol. I (Juz 1-10)"*,
whose stated creator is **Darussalam** — a commercial publisher. It carries no licence
or rights field and was uploaded by a private individual.

An earlier version of this file argued that because no translator was named and the
resource was not marked as copyrighted, the position was merely unclear. **That
reasoning no longer holds.** The text traces to a named publisher's book. Treat it as
copyrighted unless Darussalam says otherwise.

Three glosses were modified from the source: `after what` split into `after` + `what` at
2:181, 8:6 and 13:37, so segmentation matches QUSX word-for-word. No other text was
altered.

## Hindi word-by-word text

Source: **QUL resource 44** (`hi-wbw-translation`, 70,522 records).

QUL records **no author, no data source and no metadata** for it. That is an absence of
information, not evidence that the work is unowned.

Circumstantial evidence points to **Al-Huda International**: the text closely tracks the
Urdu word-by-word compilation of Dr. Farhat Hashmi, who founded Al-Huda, and
QuranWBW.com separately credits Al-Huda International for Hindi word-by-word PDFs. This
has **not** been confirmed with them.

One candidate was checked and **ruled out**: the Hindi word-for-word of Hafiz Nazar
Ahmed, with Hindi transliteration by Understand Qur'an Academy, Hyderabad, archived as
`quran-hindi-word-for-word-hafiz-nazar-ahmed`. Two widely separated passages differ
systematically in wording and in segmentation, so it is a different work. Its "public
domain" mark was applied by an uploader rather than a rights holder in any case.

## Urdu

Not published here, but recorded because it explains the caution above.

QUL resource 93 lists `Author: Unknown, Data Source: Quran Corpus project`. A sample of
**91 words across ten ayahs** was compared against the Urdu word-by-word that
QuranWBW.com credits to **Dr. Farhat Hashmi**: **90 of 91 identical**, the one
difference being a spelling variant of the letters of `يسٓ`. Typographic errors appear
in both.

So QUL's `Data Source` field records where a file was obtained, not who wrote it. The
same caution applies to resources 44 and 564: an empty or vague author field there is
not evidence that a work is unowned.
